# Code of Conduct

> **Relayon community standards for respectful, constructive, and safe participation.**

Relayon is built through careful engineering, clear communication, and collaboration. This Code of Conduct explains the behavior expected from everyone who participates in the project and its community spaces.

Our goal is to make participation welcoming and productive while keeping discussions focused on the work.

## 1. Our Standards

We expect all participants to:

- **Be respectful.** Treat people with dignity, patience, and professionalism.
- **Be constructive.** Focus criticism on ideas, code, decisions, and behavior—not personal characteristics.
- **Assume good faith.** Ask for clarification before attributing hostile intent.
- **Communicate clearly.** Provide useful context, reproducible steps, and actionable feedback.
- **Respect different experience levels.** Help newcomers where practical; do not use expertise to intimidate or belittle others.
- **Accept technical disagreement.** Discuss evidence, trade-offs, and project requirements without making it personal.
- **Protect privacy and security.** Do not publish credentials, private information, customer data, or uncoordinated vulnerability details.
- **Respect project boundaries.** Follow repository contribution procedures and the applicable project license.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Giving specific, considerate code-review feedback.
- Explaining why a proposed change creates a compatibility, reliability, or security risk.
- Acknowledging mistakes and correcting inaccurate statements.
- Respectfully disagreeing and accepting a maintainer's final project decision.
- Helping make issue reports and documentation understandable to others.

## 2. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or deliberate disruption.
- Discriminatory, hateful, or demeaning language or conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, humiliation, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Sexualized comments, advances, or content directed at another participant without consent.
- Publishing or threatening to publish another person's private information (doxxing).
- Trolling, deliberate provocation, or sustained bad-faith arguments that prevent productive discussion.
- Spam, impersonation, fraudulent claims, or malicious links.
- Sharing malware, destructive code, or instructions intended to compromise other participants or their systems.
- Publicly exposing secrets, credentials, private customer data, or sensitive security details.
- Retaliating against someone for making a good-faith report or participating in an investigation.
- Repeatedly ignoring reasonable moderation instructions.

Strong technical criticism is allowed. Disagreement, rejection of a proposal, and firm code review are not violations by themselves. The manner and context of communication matter.

## 3. Where This Code Applies

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Relayon project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews.
- Project documentation and contribution-related communications.
- Project-related chat, community channels, and public support spaces.
- Events or meetings organized by the project.
- Other situations where someone is acting as a project representative.

It applies equally to maintainers, contributors, issue reporters, reviewers, and community members.

Project spaces include public and private communications when they are directly related to Relayon participation or project activity.

## 4. Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code:

1. If it is safe to do so, preserve relevant context such as links, dates, messages, or screenshots.
2. Report the concern privately to the project maintainer using the contact details in the repository's [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE), or through an available private GitHub contact channel.
3. Include what happened, where it happened, who was involved (if known), and any relevant evidence.
4. Explain whether there is an immediate safety, privacy, or security risk.

Please do not turn a sensitive report into a public argument or publish another person's private information.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible. Information may need to be shared with people responsible for reviewing or addressing the report. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

For suspected security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](./docs/SECURITY.md) rather than posting exploit details in a public issue. If that file is unavailable in your checkout, use the repository's current security-reporting instructions.

## 5. Review and Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Where practical, reports will be reviewed fairly, based on available context and without assuming that an allegation is automatically proven.

Depending on the severity, pattern, impact, and context, responses may include:

1. **Clarification or guidance** — explaining the relevant expectation and asking for the behavior to stop.
2. **Warning** — a direct notice that the conduct is unacceptable and must not continue.
3. **Temporary restriction** — limiting participation in an issue, discussion, review, or other project space.
4. **Removal of content** — hiding or removing content that violates this Code, where the platform allows it.
5. **Temporary suspension** — restricting participation for a defined period.
6. **Permanent exclusion** — removing access to project participation spaces for serious or repeated violations.

The maintainer may choose an appropriate response without following this list in order. Serious threats, doxxing, malicious activity, or significant security risks may warrant immediate restrictions.

Enforcement decisions should consider:

- The nature and seriousness of the conduct.
- Whether it was intentional or repeated.
- Its effect on affected participants and the project.
- Relevant context and available evidence.
- Whether the participant acknowledges the issue and changes their behavior.

Retaliation against reporters, affected participants, or people assisting with a good-faith review may itself result in enforcement action.

## 6. Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Model the standards described in this document.
- Communicate project decisions and moderation requests respectfully.
- Review reports in good faith and avoid unnecessary disclosure of sensitive details.
- Apply this Code consistently, taking context and severity into account.
- Explain enforcement decisions to affected participants where doing so is appropriate and safe.
- Avoid using moderation authority to punish good-faith disagreement or criticism.

Maintainers are also participants and are expected to follow this Code.

## 7. Scope and Limitations

This Code of Conduct governs community behavior. It does not:

- Grant permission to use, modify, distribute, or deploy Relayon beyond the rights provided by its license.
- Replace the repository's contribution guidelines, security policy, or license.
- Guarantee that every report can be independently verified or resolved.
- Create an employment, agency, partnership, or other legal relationship.

The repository's [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) remains authoritative for software use and contributions. The contribution process is described in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./docs/CONTRIBUTING.md).

## 8. Acknowledgment

By participating in Relayon project spaces, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and cooperate with reasonable moderation actions.

Thank you for helping keep Relayon respectful, secure, and focused on building reliable software.
